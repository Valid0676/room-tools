How detection works

Every 40 ms the tool takes an FFT of the mic signal and finds narrow peaks. A peak becomes a candidate when it is:

  • Prominent: higher than the spectrum around it (±~1/6 octave) by at least the Prominence setting.
  • Persistent: still there at the same frequency for at least the Must persist time.
  • Pure: without strong 2nd/3rd harmonics. Voices and instruments have harmonics; feedback is close to a sine.
  • Growing or steady: feedback rises in a straight line in dB over time. Growth adds to the score.

Amber RINGING means likely; red FEEDBACK means all checks agree. Both go into the log. Run pink noise or speech through the system during ring-out if you want fewer false hits from steady music notes.

The mic's calibration file isn't needed: only the frequency matters here, not absolute level. Browser echo cancelling, noise suppression and auto gain are switched off for the input.
